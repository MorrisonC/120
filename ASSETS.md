# Open-Source 3D Asset Catalog

All visual 3D assets used in Loopkeeper 3D follow a **3D Stylized Low-Poly / Cel-Shaded** aesthetic inspired by *Cat Quest* and top-down *Zelda* titles. All asset packs listed below use the **CC0 (Public Domain)** license, free for commercial and personal use with no attribution required.

## 1. Overworld, Biomes & Nature
* **Kenney Nature Kit (3D)**
  * URL: https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
  * Author: Kenney
  * License: CC0 (Public Domain)
  * Role: Stylized trees, oak foliage, grassy knolls, rock formations, fallen logs, and dirt pathways building out the contiguous overworld biomes.

* **Kenney Fantasy Town Kit (3D)**
  * URL: https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-town-kit
  * Author: Kenney
  * License: CC0 (Public Domain)
  * Role: Modular wooden houses, roofs, market stalls, chimney smoke, signposts, and village notice boards for Hearth Clearing / Overworld Village.

* **Quaternius Ultimate Modular Ruins Pack (3D)**
  * URL: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularruins.html
  * Author: Quaternius
  * License: CC0 (Public Domain)
  * Role: Ancient overgrown pillars, stone arches, weathered pedestals for Waypoint Shrines, and broken stone bridges for loop shortcut gating.

## 2. Dungeons, Crypts & Mechanical Spire
* **Kenney Modular Dungeon Kit (3D)**
  * URL: https://kenney.nl/assets/modular-dungeon-kit
  * Author: Kenney
  * License: CC0 (Public Domain)
  * Role: 3D flagstone corridors, iron portcullises, wall torches, rotating spike obstacles, levers, and locked gates for dungeons and crypts.

* **Quaternius Fantasy Props MegaKit (3D)**
  * URL: https://quaternius.com/packs/fantasypropsmegakit.html
  * Author: Quaternius
  * License: CC0 (Public Domain)
  * Role: Treasure chests (closed/open), brass gears, ancient sundial obelisks, keys, swords, and breakable clay pots.

## 3. Hero, NPCs & Monsters (Rigged & Animated)
* **KayKit Adventurers Character Pack (3D)**
  * URL: https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-adventurers
  * Author: Kay Lousberg
  * License: CC0 (Public Domain)
  * Role: Low-poly hero models (Knight, Rogue, Mage) with standard humanoid rigs, suited for top-down 3/4 isometric perspective.

* **KayKit Character Animations (3D)**
  * URL: https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-character-animations
  * Author: Kay Lousberg
  * License: CC0 (Public Domain)
  * Role: 150+ humanoid animations including sword swings, dash-rolls, interaction pickup, cheering, idle bobs, and running cycles.

* **KayKit Skeletons & Undead Mob Pack (3D)**
  * URL: https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-skeletons
  * Author: Kay Lousberg
  * License: CC0 (Public Domain)
  * Role: Animated skeleton warriors, archers, and mages to serve as zoned patrol enemies and dungeon sentinels.

* **Quaternius Ultimate Monsters Pack (glTF)**
  * URL: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemonsters.html
  * Author: Quaternius
  * License: CC0 (Public Domain)
  * Role: 3D Starter Boss and monster models (`res://assets/models/enemies/`), rig and animation clips for crawling, hopping, burrowing, and spitting attacks.
