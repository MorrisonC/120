# ASSET_LINKS_ADDENDUM.md

Supplements the existing `ASSET_LINKS.md` with 3D open-source asset recommendations for the **3D Stylized Low-Poly ARPG** aesthetic (inspired by *Cat Quest* and top-down *Zelda* titles).

All assets listed below follow the **CC0 (Public Domain)** license, allowing free commercial and personal use.

## Primary 3D Asset Packs

1. **Kenney — Nature Kit (3D)** (CC0)
   - URL: https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
   - Use: Low-poly trees, rocks, terrain tiles, pathways across contiguous biomes.

2. **Kenney — Fantasy Town Kit (3D)** (CC0)
   - URL: https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-town-kit
   - Use: Modular buildings, village roofs, market stalls, and notice boards for Overworld Village.

3. **Quaternius — Ultimate Modular Ruins Pack (3D)** (CC0)
   - URL: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularruins.html
   - Use: Stone pillars, arches, waypoint shrines, and ruins geometry.

4. **Kenney — Modular Dungeon Kit (3D)** (CC0)
   - URL: https://kenney.nl/assets/modular-dungeon-kit
   - Use: Dungeon corridors, gates, spike obstacles, levers, and doors.

5. **Quaternius — Fantasy Props MegaKit (3D)** (CC0)
   - URL: https://quaternius.com/packs/fantasypropsmegakit.html
   - Use: Treasure chests, keys, weapons, pots, and puzzle items.

6. **KayKit — Adventurers, Animations & Skeletons (3D)** (CC0)
   - URL: https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-adventurers
   - Use: Rigged low-poly characters, combat/action animations, and enemy models.
